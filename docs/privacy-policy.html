Privacy Policy

App: SMS (package com.dns.sms) · Last updated: 21 November 2026

SMS is a messaging app that you can set as your phone's default SMS app. It sends and receives your text messages and organizes them into folders, reminders, orders and bank balances. All of this is done on your phone. We have no servers, no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we never sell or share your data.

What the app accesses and why

Data the app creates

The app keeps a small index on your phone (folder choices, stars, pins, drafts, parsed balances, reminders and orders, scheduled messages). It is stored in an encrypted database whose key is protected by Android Keystore. Balances are hidden until you unlock them with your fingerprint, face or screen lock.

Backup (optional)

If you choose a Google account in Settings → Backup & restore, the app saves a backup to that account's private Google Drive app folder (drive.appdata; the app cannot see your other Drive files). The backup is encrypted on your phone before upload (AES-256-GCM). The key is kept on your phone and in Google's end-to-end encrypted Block Store, protected by your screen lock, so neither we nor Google can read the backup. You can delete the backup from the app at any time.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or profiling.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of messages or any personal data.
  • No message content in logs. The app does not upload crash reports. If the app crashes, a short technical log (error type and code location, without message text) is kept on your phone; it is only sent if you tap "Share crash log" in Settings → About and choose where to send it.
  • Links in messages never open by themselves; the full address is shown first.

Your choices and deleting data

  • Switch to another SMS app at any time; this app then stops reading or sending messages.
  • Settings → Message rules can delete old one-time codes, promotions and blocked messages (off by default).
  • To remove everything the app created on your phone, open Android Settings → Apps → SMS → Storage → Clear storage. Your SMS remain in Android's message store unless you delete them.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its data from your phone. Delete the Drive backup from the app first if you want that removed too.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
